Week 1 objective

Flexible by baseline results.

Focus

Build the sound engine

👂 Produce a small group of consonant sounds automatically.
🍎 Produce short a and i.
🔎 Identify the first sound in simple spoken words.
🧩 Begin blending 2–3 spoken phonemes.
👏 Begin segmenting simple words.
Parent rules

Keep it playful

Keep sessions around 15–20 minutes, change activities every few minutes, allow movement, and stop positively if frustration rises.

Use pure sounds: /mmmm/, not “muh”; /ssss/, not “suh”; keep /t/ and /p/ short.

Roadmap

Approximate, not a deadline.

Weeks 1–4

Sounds → blending

Baseline, consonant and short-vowel sounds, beginning/ending sounds, oral blending and oral segmenting.

Weeks 3–8

CVC reading + spelling

Connect sounds to written CVC words, spell with movable letters, and strengthen all short-vowel patterns.

Weeks 7–14

Digraphs + blends

Build automatic decoding, add early heart words, then introduce digraphs and initial/final blends.

Weeks 12–20+

Stories + independence

Read decodable sentences and short stories, then increase fluency and independent reading.
