Notifications

Get a browser notification when it's time to start marinating, start cooking, and when a meal is ready — timed so you eat at consistent times daily. Notifications only fire while this tab is open in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari on Mac all support this). Heads up: iOS Safari on iPhone does not support web notifications for regular sites, so this won't reach your phone unless you're on desktop or Android Chrome with the tab open — a real "closed app" push to your phone would need a backend server, which this static app intentionally doesn't have.
